Lines & Trains

Drag locomotives onto lines to add trains.

How to Play

Connect stations by dragging between them to create subway lines. Trains will automatically travel along each line, carrying passengers to stations that match their shapes.

  • Passengers wait at stations; deliver them before stations overcrowd.
  • Lines crossing the river consume tunnels. If you run out of tunnels you cannot cross until you earn more.
  • Every in-game week you may choose an upgrade. Pause with Space, adjust speed with +/-.
  • Pick Sandbox/Easy, Medium, or Hard before you begin a run.
  • Toggle colorblind mode, audio, and dark mode in Settings.
  • Press D to show debug stats.
